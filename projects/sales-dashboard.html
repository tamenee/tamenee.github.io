BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE CASE STUDY

Sales Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end business analytics project using SQL, Excel and Power BI to evaluate revenue, profitability, product performance, customer segments and regional sales trends.

Portfolio Project Business Analytics
SQL Excel Power BI Data Cleaning Dashboard Design Business Intelligence

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding what drives revenue and profit

The purpose of this project is to help business leaders understand overall sales performance and identify areas requiring further attention.

The analysis examines revenue, profit, product categories, customer segments, regional performance and monthly trends. It is designed to answer both high-level management questions and detailed operational questions.

The final output is an interactive Power BI dashboard supported by SQL analysis and a documented data-cleaning process.

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Management needs a clearer view of performance

The organisation records sales transactions, but raw transaction data does not immediately explain which products, customers or regions are performing well.

Management needs a consolidated view that distinguishes revenue growth from actual profitability and identifies where attention should be focused.

Main business challenge

High sales revenue does not always produce high profit. Discounts, product costs and regional differences can create weak margins even when sales appear strong.

Questions the analysis is designed to answer

01

DATA PREPARATION

Preparing reliable data for analysis

Before creating visualisations, the data should be cleaned, validated and documented.

01

Remove duplicates

Check for repeated order lines and confirm whether duplicates represent errors or legitimate multiple-item purchases.

02

Validate missing values

Identify missing customer, product, region, sales and profit values and document how each issue is handled.

03

Standardise dates

Convert date fields into a consistent format and create year, quarter and month attributes.

04

Review numeric values

Confirm that sales, quantity, discount and profit columns use valid numeric data types.

05

Standardise categories

Correct inconsistent spelling, capitalisation and naming in product, segment and regional fields.

06

Create calculated fields

Add profit margin, average order value, monthly growth and other required measures.

SQL ANALYSIS

Example queries used in the project

These example queries demonstrate the type of analysis that will support the dashboard. Table and column names can be adjusted to match the final dataset.

SQL QUERY 01

Monthly sales and profit 

-- Group sales and profit by calendar month.
-- DATE_TRUNC may vary depending on the SQL database used.

SELECT
    DATE_TRUNC('month', order_date) AS sales_month,
    SUM(sales) AS total_sales,
    SUM(profit) AS total_profit,
    ROUND(
        SUM(profit) / NULLIF(SUM(sales), 0) * 100,
        2
    ) AS profit_margin_percentage
FROM sales_orders
GROUP BY
    DATE_TRUNC('month', order_date)
ORDER BY
    sales_month;
SQL QUERY 02

Product profitability 

-- Identify products with high revenue but weak profit.

SELECT
    product_name,
    category,
    SUM(sales) AS total_sales,
    SUM(profit) AS total_profit,
    ROUND(
        SUM(profit) / NULLIF(SUM(sales), 0) * 100,
        2
    ) AS profit_margin_percentage
FROM sales_orders
GROUP BY
    product_name,
    category
ORDER BY
    total_sales DESC;
SQL QUERY 03

Regional ranking 

-- Rank regions according to total profit.

WITH regional_performance AS (
    SELECT
        region,
        SUM(sales) AS total_sales,
        SUM(profit) AS total_profit
    FROM sales_orders
    GROUP BY
        region
)

SELECT
    region,
    total_sales,
    total_profit,
    RANK() OVER (
        ORDER BY total_profit DESC
    ) AS profit_rank
FROM regional_performance
ORDER BY
    profit_rank;

RECOMMENDATIONS

Turning analytical findings into action

Final recommendations should be based on the measured results. The examples below show the expected style of business recommendations.

01

Review low-margin products

Investigate product costs, discount levels and pricing for products that generate strong sales but weak profit.

02

Protect high-value categories

Prioritise inventory, marketing and customer retention around consistently profitable categories.

03

Investigate regional differences

Compare shipping, discounting, product mix and operational costs across weak and strong regions.

04

Plan around seasonality

Adjust inventory and promotional planning before consistently strong or weak sales periods.
